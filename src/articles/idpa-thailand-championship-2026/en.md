# IDPA Thailand Championship Scheduled for November 2026

Thailand's competitive shooting calendar continues in November with the 2026 IDPA Thailand Championship, scheduled for Saturday, 14 November 2026.

The event will take place at Tiger Tac Shooting Range and marks the 14th anniversary of IDPA Thailand.

According to the official International Defensive Pistol Association event listing, the championship is registered as a sanctioned Tier 2 competition.

## Ten stages of competition

The championship is scheduled to feature ten stages, with a published minimum round count of 145.

Competitors will participate under International Defensive Pistol Association rules, which establish requirements for safety, equipment, scoring and competition procedures.

IDPA competition is organised around practical shooting scenarios. Participants are assessed on their ability to complete the prescribed stages accurately while complying with the sport's rules.

The discipline has a dedicated international following, with competitions held across multiple countries.

## IDPA shooting in Thailand

IDPA Thailand has been affiliated with the international organisation since 2012 and provides a point of contact for clubs and competitors participating in the discipline.

The organisation has supported regular championship activity, including the Thailand Championship held in November 2025.

This year's event continues that programme and provides another opportunity for competitors to participate in a recognised national competition.

## Event information

Competitors interested in attending should consult the official IDPA event listing for registration details, eligibility requirements and any subsequent changes.

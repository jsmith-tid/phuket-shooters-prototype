## What is IPSC Action Air?

IPSC Action Air is a competitive shooting discipline using airsoft pistols. Unlike conventional target shooting, competitors navigate a series of stages designed to assess accuracy, speed and safe equipment handling.

Participants compete under established rules, with performance measured through a combination of shooting accuracy and completion time.

Different equipment divisions allow competitors to participate using a range of permitted airsoft pistol configurations.

## Action Air in Thailand

Action Air has an established competition programme across Thailand, including regional championships and nationally recognised events.

The sport provides opportunities for competitors at different experience levels, with classifications and divisions helping structure competition.

Official competition information and results are maintained through Thailand's IPSC Action Air system.

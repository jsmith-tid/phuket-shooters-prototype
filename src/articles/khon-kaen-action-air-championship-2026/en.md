# Young Shooters Shine at Khon Kaen IPSC Action Air Championship 2026

Thailand's next generation of competitive shooting talent was on display at the Khon Kaen IPSC Action Air Championship 2026, held on 12 July at Khon Kaen University Demonstration School.

The Level II competition attracted Action Air athletes from across Thailand and provided an opportunity for younger competitors to demonstrate their progress in the sport.

## Two gold medals for Chiang Rai

Among the notable performances was that of Papatsarat Ngoenyen, a primary school student from Anuban Chiang Rai School.

Competing against other Action Air athletes, she won two gold medals, earning recognition for both her school and Chiang Rai province.

Her achievement was subsequently reported by the Chiang Rai Provincial Public Relations Office.

## Developing Thailand's next generation

Action Air competitions are an established part of Thailand's shooting-sports calendar, offering structured competitive opportunities under recognised sporting rules.

The discipline uses airsoft equipment and places emphasis on accuracy, speed, safety and consistency.

Youth competitions provide a pathway for developing athletes to gain experience, build confidence and compete at increasingly demanding levels.

The Khon Kaen championship also formed part of the wider competitive environment ahead of preparations for Thailand's next National Youth Games.

## A growing sporting community

Action Air events take place across Thailand throughout the year, with competitions hosted at dedicated ranges and other suitable venues.

The continued participation of young athletes highlights the importance of organised competitions, qualified officials and supervised sporting environments.

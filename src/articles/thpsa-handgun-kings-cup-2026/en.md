# Thailand's IPSC Handgun King's Cup Draws More Than 200 Competitors

Thailand's practical shooting community gathered in Chonburi from 4–6 September 2026 for the THPSA Handgun King's Cup, one of the country's major IPSC handgun competitions.

The Level III championship took place at the Practical Shooting Range at the Eastern National Sports Training Centre in Bang Lamung, Chonburi.

According to the national competition records, 201 competitors participated in the event.

## A major competition on the Thai calendar

The King's Cup brought together competitors participating in the structured divisions of International Practical Shooting Confederation (IPSC) handgun competition.

IPSC events differ from conventional static target shooting. Competitors complete stages designed to assess accuracy, speed and safe firearm handling under the supervision of qualified range officials.

Each competitor's performance is evaluated using the sport's established scoring rules.

Level III events represent a significant part of the competitive calendar, attracting experienced participants and providing opportunities to compete at a high standard.

## Supporting Thailand's international ambitions

The championship took place during an active period for Thai practical shooting.

National sports organisations were also preparing athletes for international shotgun competition, reflecting Thailand's involvement in several practical shooting disciplines.

Thailand regularly hosts both domestic and international competitions, with venues in Chonburi playing an important role in the country's shooting-sports programme.

## Practical shooting across Thailand

Alongside major national championships, the Thai competition calendar includes regional IPSC handgun, shotgun and Action Air events.

These competitions provide structured opportunities for athletes to measure performance, develop experience and participate in the wider shooting-sports community.

# Customer email map

The customer receipt for both individual and group enquiries uses `public/assets/images/phuket-shooters-location-map.png`, a one-time OpenStreetMap Standard map export of the area around 7.841593, 98.3562825 (Chalong, Phuket). The export bounding box was 98.352, 7.8395, 98.360, 7.844 at 1:2500, obtained on 25 October 2026 through OpenStreetMap's Share/Export form. The exported PNG is 1272 × 722 pixels and about 301 KB.

The map shows Soi Palai and nearby landmarks. The **image has no destination pin**. The linked OpenStreetMap page carries the exact coordinates and a pin; the email also gives the postal address and separate Apple Maps and Google Maps directions links using those coordinates. Check the point and entrance with the business before public release, especially for coach arrivals.

Attribution `© OpenStreetMap contributors` is visible in the lower-right corner of the image and repeated as linked text below it in the email. The map image is hosted as a first-party site asset rather than requesting community map tiles each time a recipient opens a message. The text address and navigation links remain usable when images are blocked by an email client.

# Draft enquiry privacy wording — owner review required

This is a proposed notice for the **individual and group enquiry forms**. It is deliberately not published on the forms yet: the retention period, the legal identity of the data controller, and whether the contact address handles privacy requests must reflect actual operations. The public wording should be released only after those facts are confirmed. It does not replace a site-wide privacy policy or the existing range-user records policy.

## Suggested short form notice

> Phuket Shooters uses the details you enter to review your enquiry, contact you about availability and arrangements, and send you a copy of your request. Our website and email providers process the details to deliver this service. We propose keeping enquiries that do not become bookings for **12 months after the last contact**, then deleting them unless a longer period is required for a legal obligation or dispute. For questions about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion, email **info@phuketshooters.com**. [Read the full enquiry privacy notice.]

The 12-month period is a **proposal**, not a verified current practice. Do not use this text publicly unless the business can actually apply the period to the staff mailbox and any enquiry records. If an enquiry becomes a booking, specify the separate booking-record period in the full notice.

## Suggested full enquiry notice

**Who handles your information.** [Confirm legal business/controller name], trading as Phuket Shooters, 33, 54 Soi Palai, Chalong, Mueang Phuket District, Phuket 83130, Thailand. Contact: [confirm privacy contact; proposed `info@phuketshooters.com`].

**What we collect.** We receive the contact and visit details entered on the individual or group form: name, email address, phone number where provided, preferred date and time, party or group size, coach count and organisation where provided, and any message you enter. Do not enter passport numbers or other sensitive information in the free-text field. The enquiry form does not request an ID document upload. At the range, separate record-keeping requirements may apply; those are outside this enquiry notice.

**Why and how we use it.** We use these details to review the request, check availability, discuss arrangements, reply to you and send an acknowledgement copy. A form submission is an enquiry; it is not a confirmed booking or parking reservation. [Confirm the legal basis under the Thai PDPA before publishing.]

**Who receives it.** Staff who handle enquiries can access the request. Cloudflare processes the form as the website host/security provider, and Resend sends the staff and customer emails. [Confirm any other service providers, onward transfers and existing booking-system handoff.]

**How long we keep it.** Proposed: delete enquiries that do not become bookings **12 months after the last contact**, subject to a longer period where required for a legal obligation or dispute. [Confirm that the staff mailbox, Resend account, logs and any booking-system copy can follow a documented schedule, and specify the separate booking-record period.] Provider logs and backups may have separate technical retention; describe them accurately before publication.

**Your choices and rights.** Contact [confirmed privacy address] to ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction or an objection to processing, as applicable. We may need to verify your identity before acting. [Confirm the final rights and response process with the business or adviser.]

**Maps.** The customer email loads a map image from the Phuket Shooters prototype site when your email client allows images. Selecting the map opens OpenStreetMap; separate links open Apple Maps or Google Maps. Those services handle your visit under their own privacy terms.

## Decisions before publication

1. Confirm the controller's legal name and the privacy contact address.
2. Accept or change the proposed **12 months after last contact** period for unconverted enquiries, and define when/how deletion actually happens across mailbox, Resend and other copies.
3. Define the period for requests that become bookings and for range-user records; do not imply this enquiry notice overrides those obligations.
4. Confirm any transfer or provider wording and the Thai PDPA legal basis with someone responsible for compliance.

The Thai Personal Data Protection Act, Section 23, calls for a notice at collection covering purpose, retention or expected retention, recipients, controller contact and data-subject rights. Source: [official Thai government English translation](https://pdpa.dmh.go.th/news/files/pdpa.pdf).

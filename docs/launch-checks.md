# Tour-operator launch checks

This register concerns the English, Thai and Simplified Chinese operator pages. They are currently a public **design prototype**, not a booking service. The group form only previews details in the browser; it does not send, save or reserve anything. Business owners below are proposed roles, not assigned people.

| ID | Topic | Current evidence/assumption | Consequence if wrong | Required action | Proposed owner | Status | Launch treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TO-01 | Coach parking count | Free coach parking confirmed; four spaces provisional | Groups may plan for unavailable parking | Confirm coach sizes and simultaneous parking | Unassigned business owner | Open | Omit the number if unconfirmed; retain “Free coach parking” in all locales |
| TO-02 | Visitor capacity | Unknown; parking capacity does not establish visitor throughput | Unsafe or unserviceable group expectations | Confirm scheduling, simultaneous participants and maximum group size | Unassigned operations owner | Open | Publish no numeric visitor-capacity claim |
| TO-03 | Visit duration | 1–4 hours provisional | Itineraries may be unrealistic | Confirm typical durations by activity and group size | Unassigned operations owner | Open | If unconfirmed, replace with a contact-to-plan message in all three locales |
| TO-04 | Coach approach and map | Coaches can park; entrance and route details unconfirmed | Incorrect directions or difficult arrival | Confirm approach, turning/drop-off arrangements and need for an annotated map | Unassigned operations owner | Open | Use only the existing location and generic directions link |
| TO-05 | Booking process | Request, staff review and explicit manual confirmation are proposed, not operationally agreed | Visitor may mistake an enquiry for a reservation | Agree lead time, response owner, confirmation and shared record | Unassigned business owner | Open | Prototype form must continue to say nothing is sent or booked |
| TO-06 | Payment terms | Cash on day or card payment arranged by phone confirmed | Incorrect payment expectations | Confirm payer, timing, deposits, supported cards/currencies, cancellations and refunds | Unassigned business owner | Open | Publish only the two known methods; collect no card details |
| TO-07 | Trade arrangements | Rates, commission and guide/driver benefits unknown | Unapproved commercial promises | Decide whether any offers exist | Unassigned business owner | Open | Omit offers and discounts |
| TO-08 | Guest requirements | Group-specific age, ID, accessibility and other requirements not established in this brief | Guests may arrive unprepared | Confirm approved policies and accessible arrangements | Unassigned operations owner | Open | Reuse only separately approved existing policy |
| TO-09 | Translation review | Thai and Chinese copy is draft | Misleading or unnatural visitor information | Arrange fluent business review and reconcile all three locales | Unassigned content owner | Open | Do not release as approved copy until reviewed |
| TO-10 | Real request delivery | No backend; group form is intentionally a dummy | Real enquiries would be lost | Specify and build a secure delivery service before accepting requests | Unassigned technical owner | Deferred | Never label the prototype form as operational |
| TO-11 | Delivery operations | Sender, inbox, provider and hosting not configured | Staff cannot receive requests | Choose recipient, verified sender, provider, hosting and delivery monitoring | Unassigned business/technical owners | Deferred | No real submission or receipt claims |
| TO-12 | Manual confirmation | Staff ownership and booking record not agreed | Requests may not be followed up or recorded | Assign request monitoring, confirmation and change handling | Unassigned operations owner | Open | Promise no response time; do not automate confirmation |
| TO-13 | Personal data | No real group submission or storage now | A future live form may mishandle contact details | Agree retention, access, privacy wording, validation and abuse protection before enabling delivery | Unassigned business/technical owners | Deferred | Keep current form local-only; no analytics payloads with form values |

Fallback for TO-03 if duration remains unconfirmed: “Contact us to plan the time needed for your group and chosen activities.” Obtain reviewed Thai and Simplified Chinese equivalents before changing the public copy.
